# What "Documentary" is for — Emerge X editing style

**In one line:** a premium streaming-documentary look where real archive footage carries the picture and quiet, expensive-looking scenes — maps, real headlines, photo spotlights, cut-out objects, calm cream-and-gold serif graphics — land exactly on the words.

## Best for these niches
- **History & "how it happened"** — the true story of a war, a disaster, a treaty, a company's rise; real footage plus a map when a place matters and a dateline on the date.
- **Business & brand stories** — the fall of a retailer, how a founder built (or wrecked) a company; real photos of the people, cut-out products, a bar chart on warm paper.
- **Crime & scandal (reported, not lurid)** — court cases, frauds, falls from grace told with real news footage and the real article highlighted.
- **Sport history** — a rivalry, a scandal, a record; real match footage, photos of the athletes, a stats graphic.
- **Science & space** — a mission, a discovery, an engineering feat told with real footage and quiet figure graphics.
- **Biographies** — a life told end to end: real photos of the person at each stage, dates as datelines, quotes in serif.
- **Investigations / exposés** — "what really happened to X", built on real pages, real places, real objects.
- **Geopolitics & conflict explainers** — a region's crisis told soberly with maps, headlines and archive footage.

## Example video titles it fits
- "The Night the Lusitania Sank"
- "How Nokia Lost a Billion Customers"
- "The Enron Tapes: Inside the Collapse"
- "The Rise and Fall of Concorde"
- "What Actually Happened at Chernobyl"
- "The Man Who Sold the Eiffel Tower — Twice"
- "Why the Berlin Airlift Nearly Started a War"

## Also works for
- **True-crime documentaries** with a restrained, factual tone (for a darker case-file feel instead → see below).
- **Tech company stories** where you have real footage and photos rather than product demos.
- **Music / film-industry history** of the recent, well-documented era.
- **Nature & exploration** stories built on real expedition footage.

## Not the right style for
- Pre-photography or footage-less history → use **Style 2 - 3D History**.
- Reflective psychology / mindset essays over atmosphere → use **Style 3 - Dark Psychology**.
- A tense, forensic case-file treatment → use **Style 8 - True Crime**.
- Fast, high-energy facts or list videos → use **Style 21 - High-Retention Facts** or **Style 30 - Top 10 Countdown**.
- A second-person life story in drawn pictures → use **Style 5 - POV Story**.
- A talking-head opener that then shows pictures → use **Style 6 - AI Presenter**.

## How to recognise it (the look)
- Palette: cream ink (#F3EEE4) and a gold accent (#E3B25B) over natural, slightly-denser footage; near-clean picture, no vignette.
- Fonts: EB Garamond serif for figures, dates and quotes; Roboto Condensed caps for kickers and labels.
- Pacing: a new real shot about every 4 s; the first 60 s is the most-edited stretch, then one quiet graphic roughly every 45 s.
- Captions: still (no karaoke), Roboto Condensed 46 px, white, bottom-centre, one line.
- Signatures: animated maps, highlighted real newspaper pages, photo push-ins on named people, cut-out objects flying in, short paper-collage moments, counting-up figures, soft whooshes felt not heard.

## What you supply vs what the style does
- **You supply (content):** the topic or script, and the names, places, dates and real sources behind it — this style wants **real archive/news footage, real photos of the people, real pages and real objects**. It does not use stock or AI pictures by default.
- **The style decides (editing):** which shot cuts where, when a map/headline/spotlight/graphic appears and on which word, the grade, the captions, the labels, the sound design and the final mix.
