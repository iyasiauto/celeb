# Documentary — edit playbook

How a senior editor cuts THIS style, clip by clip: a premium streaming documentary built from real footage, with
quiet scenes placed exactly on the words. Values are at 1920×1080, 30 fps. Read EDITING-STYLE.md for the look; this
file decides the edit.

## 1. The hook (first 60 s), second by second

Cut rate: hook ≈ one visual change every 2.5–3.5 s (footage slots ~3–4 s, scenes 4.6–7 s, labels between them);
body ≈ one real shot every ~4 s, a scene every ~10–15 s, at least 6 s of plain footage between scenes.

| Time | On screen (visual type) | Shot length | Effect | Transition | Text on screen | SFX / music |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0–1 s | The strongest real picture of the story: the footage of the moment itself (never a map, never a title) | first shot 2.5–4 s | 3.5 % slow push, base grade | none — frame 1 is picture | none (captions start with the voice) | deep hit on frame 1; bed fades in over 2.5 s |
| 1–3 s | Same shot or a second angle of the same moment | 2–3 s | push continues | hard cut | captions; a place/date stamp top-left if the first sentence names one | — |
| 3–7 s | Map of the first real place (region or pin) — or a dateline if no place | 7 s (starts 1.6 s before the word) | camera flies in, place lifts out | hard cut + whoosh | map title + subtitle (the date rides here) | whoosh in, hit at +1.1 s |
| 7–15 s | Footage of the event, sentence by sentence; photo spotlight on the first public figure named | footage 3–4 s; spotlight 5 s | spotlight: subject cut out, rest darkens and blurs | hard cuts; whoosh into the spotlight | name label (bottom-left) on the spotlight or the next shot | whoosh on spotlight |
| 15–30 s | Match cut on the first naming of the main person (6–10 real photos) → footage → paper-collage moment (7–11 s) → footage | match cut 4.6 s; collage 7–11 s | flash + push per photo; collage springs and drifts | hard cuts; white flash inside the match cut | labels on footage between scenes (figure / date) | shutter per cut, riser into the last photo; paper on the collage |
| 30–60 s | Real headline page on the first reported fact; a cut-out object if a product is central; a graphic (number or dateline); one sound-up (real clip with its own sound); footage between | page 6.5 s; object 4.6–5.6 s; graphic 5–6 s; sound-up 2–4 s | marker sweep; object spring; figure counts up | hard cuts; 2 s of footage between scenes is enough | ≥5 labels in the minute in total | paper + hit on page; whoosh + hit on graphic; whoosh in / hit out around the sound-up |

Must be on screen by 3 s: real footage of the event and the first caption. By 10 s: the map of the first place
(or a dateline) and at least one label. By 30 s: the match cut, a photo spotlight and a collage moment. By 60 s: a
real headline page, a graphic, a sound-up, ≥5 labels, and scenes covering up to 62 % of the minute.
A video under 90 s opens on footage, a spotlight or the real article, never on a map (the map comes later and short).

## 2. Visual sequence pattern

Codes: V = real footage, P = photo spotlight / photo, O = cut-out object, C = paper collage, G = graphic, M = map,
H = headline page, X = match cut / text match cut, S = sound-up, L = label over V (not a slot of its own).

- Hook pattern (0–60 s): `V M V P V X V C V H V O G S V` — every scene separated by ≥2 s of V.
- Typical body minute: `V V C V V P V V G V V H V L V` (with 3–5 L over the V's).
- Target share of screen time — hook: V 38–45 %, scenes ≤62 %. Body: V 58–65 %, C ~12–15 %, P+O ~8–10 %,
  G ~8–10 %, H ~6–8 %, M ~3–5 %.
- Max same type in a row: V ×4 (then a scene or a label must arrive); any scene type ×1 (never two scenes of the same
  type back to back, never two scenes without footage between them).
- Minimum gaps: two graphics ≥30 s (target one every ~45 s); two collages ≥45 s (target ~1 per minute in the body,
  1 per 150 s in a long quiet video); two maps ≥15 s (average one per 35 s at most); two photo/object scenes ≥3 s
  (average one per 25 s); two headline pages ≥25 s; satellite dives ≥150 s apart.
- Always follows: every scene is followed by V (≥6 s in the body, ≥2 s in the hook). A map is followed by footage of
  that place. A headline page is followed by footage of the event it reports. A spotlight is followed by footage of that
  person when it exists.
- Chapter pattern (each ~2.5 min stretch of the body): opens on V (establishing footage of the new place/time) → M or
  G dateline if place/time changed → body minute pattern ×2–3 → closes on a strong V or C before the next stretch.

## 3. Clip decision rules — "look at the clip, then decide"

Before placing any clip, look at 3 frames (start, middle, end). Every shot must show what the sentence under it says.

| Clip situation | Shot length | Cut in / out | Transition in | Transition out | Effect / motion | Colour | SFX |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Fast-action footage (crash, goal, launch) | 2.2–3.5 s | in on the action's start or mid-action; out as the next action starts | hard cut | hard cut | 3.5 % push only | base grade | none (or a sound-up if it has great native sound and sits at a sentence end) |
| Slow / static footage (room, landscape, parked object) | 3.5–5 s (never >7 s) | in on the first word of its sentence | hard cut | hard cut on the next sentence's first word | 3.5 % push gives it life | base | none |
| Drone / wide establishing shot | 4–5 s | at the start of a new place or chapter | hard cut | hard cut | push 3.5 % | base | none; a map may follow if the place is new |
| Close-up of a face (public figure) | 3–4 s | on the sentence naming them | hard cut | hard cut | push | protect skin (see §5) | none; name label rides on it |
| Archive / old low-res footage | 3–4.5 s | cut away before artefacts or burned text appear | hard cut | hard cut | push; crop black bars and sprockets; never upscale-sharpen; film-dirt bed 0.35 | archive correction §5 | none |
| Vertical 9:16 or 4:3 clip | 3–4 s | — | hard cut | hard cut | cover-crop to fill 16:9 (keep the subject centred); never side bars | base | none |
| Burned-in text / channel logo | as needed | trim to the clean stretch | hard cut | hard cut | crop the logo corner away; if impossible push-in 1.24× (1.28× on a static shot); drop the clip if text covers the subject | base | none |
| Dark / night clip | 3–5 s | — | hard cut | hard cut | push | lift shadows ~+4 %, keep blacks deep; never grey | none; drop a clip that reads as a black screen |
| Very bright clip (snow, sky, white studio) | 3–4 s | — | hard cut | hard cut | push | pull highlights −5 %; labels get the blurred shade | none |
| Still photo of a person (public) | 5 s spotlight | starts 0.9 s before the name | hard cut + whoosh | hard cut | subject cut out, rest darkens / desaturates / blurs as the camera pushes in | photo correction §5 | whoosh |
| Photo of a place / object | object 4.6–5.6 s | object has landed 0.5 s before its name | hard cut + whoosh | hard cut | object cut out, rises turning, overshoots, settles, drop shadow | own colours on blurred ground | whoosh |
| Document / newspaper / web article | 6.5 s headline page | starts 1.3 s before the claim's words; the marker starts ON the words | hard cut + paper | hard cut | tilted window, push-in, marker sweep line by line, DOF | page as is | paper, quiet hit at +1.4 s |
| Low-res image (<900 px wide) | — | — | — | — | do not use as a spotlight; use it only small inside a collage, or replace with a label | — | — |
| Two clips of the same subject back to back | 3–4 s each | change angle AND scale (wide → close), never the same framing | hard cut | hard cut | if it is the same shot, it must be ≥30 s later, from later in the clip, reframed 1.25× on one side | base | none |
| Motion left→right | 3–4 s | next shot moving the same direction (or static); never a head-on direction flip unless the story turns | hard cut | hard cut | push | base | none |
| A number / figure is spoken | graphic 6 s or label 2.2–4.5 s | the figure lands on the word (graphic starts 0.15 s before) | hard cut + whoosh | words leave in last 0.5 s | counts up 0.35–2.2 s | — | whoosh, hit at +2.2 s |
| A name is spoken | spotlight 5 s or name label 3.6 s | label 0.1 s before the name; leaves 1.2 s after the sentence | fade 260 ms | fade 320 ms | label slides 24 px in 0.42 s, gold rule wipes | — | none for labels |
| A date / place is spoken | dateline 5 s, map 7 s or stamp 3.6 s | on the word | hard cut / fade | — | dateline types on; map flies in 1.6 s | — | whoosh + hit at 0.95 s (dateline) |
| Emotional / sad line | hold the footage 4–5 s; no scene | let the sentence finish on the same shot | hard cut | hard cut | push only, no labels over it; a warm leak (screen 0.5) may drift over it (max 2 per video) | base | music breathes — no SFX (a soft whoosh out only if a leak arrives on the cut) |
| Reveal / twist line | pivot graphic 4.6 s or a strong real shot | the cut lands on the twist word | hard cut + whoosh | — | pivot line rises line by line | — | whoosh + hit at 0.35 s |
| Light / ironic moment | 2.5–3.5 s footage | cut on the punch word | hard cut | hard cut | none extra | base | none (the style stays calm) |
| A list ("three reasons") | footage per item, or a timeline / chart graphic | a new shot on each item's first word | hard cut | hard cut | timeline events tap in one by one | — | taps per item |
| A question to the viewer | question graphic 4.2 s (≤6 words) | on the question | hard cut | — | gold box wipes open 0.2–0.85 s | — | riser into it + hit |
| Chapter change (new stretch, new place/time) | establishing V 4–5 s → map or dateline | on the first word of the new chapter | hard cut | — | — | — | whoosh into the map / dateline |
| The last 10 s | the payoff: the strongest real image left, held 4–6 s; optionally a collage or pivot just before | end on picture, not on a graphic | hard cut | music fades over 4 s under the last words | push | base | bed resolves; no hit at the end |

Cutting rules:
- Cut on the spoken word: a shot plays while its own sentence is spoken; a new shot starts on the first word of a new
  thing named. Captions, labels and scenes are timed on the voice's own word clock, not estimated from letters.
- Cut on action: leave a shot as its action starts; open the next mid-action from a new angle.
- Never cut mid-word; a cut lands in the gap before a word, at most 0.1 s before it.
- J-cuts: the voice of the next sentence may start under the end of the previous shot by up to 0.3 s; between two
  scenes the next sentence's shot may arrive up to 5 s early when nothing else fits. L-cuts: only for sound-ups (the
  clip's own sound tails 0.2–0.4 s into the next sentence's pause) and interview-free native sound.
- Shot length: min 2.2 s (a clean stretch between the clip's own cuts), typical 3–4 s, max ~7 s. Hold longer (5–7 s)
  only for grief, awe, or a sentence that is still being spoken about that picture.
- A slot that would leave less than 2.5 s before the next scene shares the room with the next slot — the words just
  before a scene get their own shot.
- Never repeat a shot within 30 s; a repeat comes from later in the clip, reframed 1.25× left or right.
- A clip too short for its slot: slow it to at most 74 % speed (×1.35), then hold its last frame. Never loop. If
  still too short, borrow a shot of what was already said (least recently shown, not within 12 s) — never a shot of a
  sentence still to come, except a line 15–45 s ahead with more than five unseen shots may lend its weakest one.
- A sentence about the main subject with no matching shot (a rumour, a statement) takes a plain shot of that subject
  from the same period, never one tied to another dated event.

## 5. Colour grade

Base (all real footage): exposure 0; contrast +4 %; gamma 0.99 (mids a hair down); highlights and shadows untouched;
saturation −6 %; temperature and tint neutral; a gentle, almost linear curve; the look is "natural colour, slightly
denser and calmer than the source" — no teal-orange, no LUT. Grain: none added to footage (archive brings its own).
Vignette: none. Sharpen: none.

    eq=contrast=1.04:saturation=0.94:gamma=0.99

Per-source corrections (before the base grade):
- Modern HD footage: base only; if it is punchy/oversaturated (sports broadcasts, ads) add saturation −6 % more.
- Old archive footage: never sharpen; lift black point slightly (`curves=all='0/0.02 1/1'`), keep its grain; if the
  colour has faded magenta or cyan, neutralise with `colorbalance` (±0.03–0.06 on shadows/mids); crop bars.
- Stills / photos: match mids to the surrounding footage (±5 % brightness); black-and-white photos stay black-and-white.
- Illustrated / AI stills: not used in this style.
- Very dark clips: `eq=brightness=0.03:gamma=1.08` before the base, keep blacks at 16; if it still reads as black, drop it.
- Very bright clips: `eq=brightness=-0.03`, `curves=highlights` pulled −5 %; never crush to grey.
- Mismatched clips side by side: match the second to the first on mid-grey luma and white balance first
  (`colorbalance` on midtones ±0.05), then apply the base grade to both — never grade them separately to taste.
- Skin tones: never shift hue on skin; any saturation cut above −10 % must be offset in the 10–40° hue band.

## 6. Photo & collage treatments

- Single photo: photo spotlight (above) — never a plain Ken Burns on a photo in this style. The cutout stays sharp;
  the ground goes dim, desaturated and soft; push ~8–10 % over 5 s on the subject, ease-out.
- Match cut: 6–10 photos, face locked to the same spot (centre x 50 %, y 44 %, face height 36 % of frame), a blurred
  28 px, 55 %-brightness copy of the photo fills the frame behind tight portraits; ~0.3–0.6 s per photo, the last held.
- Paper collage (the only collage): one shared stage per video — an aged archival map / newsprint texture in tan
  #C9BB9C with grain and halftone. Per moment: 2–4 shots of ~3.4 s. Layout per shot: one hero cutout (black-and-white
  halftone, white keyline, offset red #D62E1F stroke) at 40–60 % frame height, 1–3 supporting cards (typewriter label
  strip, rubber stamp, big red number card, torn archival photo) — cards rotated −6° to +6°, soft drop shadows (offset
  ~8 px, blur ~16 px, 35 %), masking-tape scraps, brass pins and red string for connections. Entrance order: the hero
  springs up first (overshoot, settle in ~0.5 s), cards follow 0.2–0.4 s apart, each landing on the word that names
  it; a red underline swipes left → right under the key word. Hold each finished shot ≥1.2 s; no shot under 2 s.
  Camera: ~2 % drift across the tabletop, a micro push in, never closer than 2×. Real people keep their real faces
  (no eye bars); private people are never drawn. Words sit above the pictures; filler words never become a card.
- No grids, split screens or picture-in-picture: this style never tiles footage.

## 7. Transitions — which one for which cut

| Situation | Transition | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| Footage → footage (any) | hard cut | 0 |
| Footage → graphic / map / spotlight / object | hard cut + whoosh peaking on the cut | 0 |
| Graphic → footage | words ease out over the last 0.5 s, picture holds, hard cut | 0.5 s text-out |
| Map → footage | hard cut + soft whoosh out | 0 |
| Headline page in / out | hard cut; the window rises in over ~1.3 s inside the scene | — |
| Inside a match cut | white flash | 0.13 s decay |
| Inside a collage | hard cuts; now and then a paper wipe or torn-edge reveal | 0.3–0.5 s |
| Sound-up | hard cut in and out | 0 |
| Label on / off | fade | 260 ms in / 320 ms out |
| Captions | fade | 170 ms |

About 97 % of all cuts are plain hard cuts. Transition + SFX pairs: scene in = whoosh; scene out = soft whoosh;
page = paper; match cut = shutter; collage = paper; figure landing = low hit. Never: dissolves between clips, dips to
black or white, light leaks, glitches, zoom or spin transitions, star wipes.

## 8. Text & captions timing

- Captions: a line appears on its first word (0 frames early) and leaves as the next line's first word begins
  (−2 frames); 170 ms fades; one line, ≤48 chars; hidden under graphics, maps, pages and collages.
- Labels: start 3 frames (0.1 s) before the word; fade in 8 frames; hold 2.2–4.5 s; leave 0.3 s after the sentence
  ends (a name 1.2 s after); fade out 10 frames. One label at a time; ≥0.25 s between two.
- Graphics: land their key element on the word (graphic starts 0.15 s = 5 frames early); the figure finishes counting
  ~2.2 s in.
- Maps start 1.6 s (48 frames) before the place word; spotlights 0.9 s; objects 0.5 s; headline pages 1.3 s.
- Never captioned or labelled: filler words, the narrator's sentence retyped as a label, a private person's face, text
  over graphics, maps or pages. Never two text elements besides the caption at once.

## 9. Sound on the edit

Levels vs the voice's speech loudness (tested values):
- Voice: 80 Hz high-pass, compression −18 dB / 2.5:1 / 8 ms / 120 ms; pauses of 1.5–2 s after a blow, 0.3–0.6 s before a beat.
- Bed: −6 dB vs voice in pauses, ducked −6 dB more under speech (≈ −12 dB; must be ≤ −10), 40 ms attack, 350 ms
  release, EQ carve −4 dB at 2.6 kHz (Q 0.9). Start the track 10–30 s in; fade out over the last 1.5–4 s.
- Whoosh (1.15 s, peak at 0.7 s) at −18 dB, placed so its PEAK lands on the cut into every scene; softer whoosh out
  (peak 0.57 s) at −23.5 dB on the cut back to footage from graphics, maps and collage.
- Low warm hit −21 dB when a figure finishes counting, −22 dB when a map pin lands, a date lands (0.95 s) or a pivot
  line lands (0.35 s).
- Riser (peak 1.5 s) at −22 dB ending exactly on a question graphic or on the last photo of the match cut.
- Shutter −17 dB on every match-cut photo; a hit under every text-cut line.
- Paper −17.5 dB on every collage shot and headline page; stamp thump −22 dB on the landing frame; typewriter keys −24 dB
  about 9 per second while a strip types; taps −21 dB on timeline dots, rows and underline sweeps.
- Deep hit −15 dB under frame 1.
- Sound-up: the clip's own sound at −1.5 dB vs the voice inside a narration pause, 60 ms in / 250 ms out, whoosh in
  (−20 dB) and hit out (−20 dB); the bed ducks under it.
- Never: SFX on footage-to-footage cuts, on captions or labels; nothing in the 2 s before a twist word; no hit at the end.
- Master: −14 LUFS integrated, true peak ≤ −1.5 dBTP (limit at −1.95 for margin).

## 10. The editing prompt (copy-paste)

```
You are the senior editor of a premium streaming-style documentary channel. Edit the video below in the
"Documentary" style. Output ONLY an EDL table.

INPUT
- SCRIPT with word timings: {SCRIPT with timings}
- CLIP LIST (id, description, duration, what it shows, quality notes): {CLIP LIST}

STYLE RULES
- Real footage carries the video. No stock, no AI pictures. A shot plays while ITS sentence is spoken; new shot on the
  first word of each new thing named; ~one shot every 4 s (2.2–7 s). Never loop; a repeat only >=30 s later,
  from later in the clip, reframed 1.25x. Never show an event before the narration reaches it.
- Scenes on the words: MAP (7 s, starts 1.6 s early), SPOTLIGHT photo (5 s, 0.9 s early), OBJECT (4.6-5.6 s, 0.5 s
  early), HEADLINE page (6.5 s, 1.3 s early), GRAPHIC (dateline 5, number 6, chart 8, timeline 8.5, quote 7.5,
  pivot 4.6, question 4.2 s), COLLAGE (7-14 s), MATCH CUT (4.6 s, once), TEXT CUT (5 s, once), SOUND-UP (once).
- Hook (0-60 s): open on the strongest real footage (no map, no title); map of the first place by ~0:07; spotlight
  on the first public figure; match cut on the first naming of the main person; a collage before 0:30; a real
  headline, a graphic and a sound-up before 1:00; >=5 labels; 2 s of footage between scenes; scenes <=62 %.
- Body: scenes <=42 %; >=6 s of footage between scenes; per minute ~1 collage, 1-2 spotlights/objects, 1 graphic,
  1-2 headlines, 3-5 labels; a map only when a new place matters. Never the same scene type twice in a row.
- Labels: names/figures bottom-left, places/dates top-left stamp, 2.2-4.5 s, start 0.1 s before the word, never over
  a scene. Captions: still, one line <=48 chars, hidden under graphics/maps/pages/collage.
- Transitions: hard cuts only (whoosh into scenes); white flash only inside the match cut.
- Grade: contrast +4 %, saturation -6 %, gamma 0.99; slow 3.5 % push on every clip; no vignette on footage.
- Overlay effects: film-dirt bed (multiply 0.35) on footage stretches only; a warm leak (screen 0.5) on the sound-up and
  the closing shot only; nothing on graphics, maps, pages, collage, match cut, spotlight or under labels.
- SFX: whoosh into scenes, low hit when figures/dates land, riser into questions, paper on pages, shutter on match cut.
  Music bed -6 dB under voice, ducks -6 dB more. Master -14 LUFS.

OUTPUT: one row per slot, in time order:
| # | start | end | narration words | visual type | source clip + in/out | effect | transition in | text on screen | SFX | note |
Visual types: V footage, P spotlight, O object, C collage, G graphic(type), M map(kind), H headline, X match/text cut,
S sound-up. In "note", say why this clip fits these words. Flag any sentence with no fitting clip.
```

## 11. Self-review before export

1. First frame is real footage of the event, not a map or title; deep hit under it.
2. By 0:10 a map of the first place (or a dateline) and a label are on screen.
3. Match cut, spotlight and collage all happen before 0:30.
4. Headline page, graphic, sound-up and ≥5 labels before 1:00.
5. Scenes ≤62 % of the hook, ≤42 % of the body; ≥6 s of footage between body scenes.
6. No two scenes of the same type in a row; no two scenes without footage between them.
7. Every shot matches its sentence; no shot appears before its event.
8. No shot over ~7 s; no shot under 2.2 s except inside match cuts / collages.
9. No looped shot; repeats ≥30 s apart and visibly reframed.
10. No watermarks, logos, presenters, black bars, or burned-in text left in any clip.
11. Grade identical across the video (contrast +4 %, sat −6 %); archive clips corrected before, not instead of, it.
12. Every graphic adds a fact the voice doesn't say, with a real source; no retyped narration.
13. Labels: right corner per kind, on the right word (±3 frames), never over a scene, max one at a time.
14. Captions one line, ≤48 chars, still, hidden under graphics/maps/pages/collage, names spelled as in the script.
15. Private people never shown as photos; every public person named in a relationship story has a photo.
16. Overlay effects only on footage: dust bed, ≤2 warm leaks; none on graphics/maps/pages/collage or under labels.
17. Whoosh on every scene cut; every SFX at its §9 level (−15 to −24 dB under the voice).
18. Bed −6 dB under voice with smooth ducking; no track jump; ends with a 4 s fade.
19. Loudness −14 LUFS integrated, peaks ≤ −1.5 dBTP; no frozen frame at the end.
