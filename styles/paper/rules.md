# Paper / Vox explainer — how it is edited

- Every sentence gets the picture that shows what it says, landing on its words ('@words' timing).
- Never two plain shots of the same kind for long: break runs of clips/photos with the template's devices.
- Every number the narrator stresses is SHOWN with a number device; every place named is SHOWN on a map; every quotation is set as a quote.
- No clip or picture twice in the same video; no talking heads, influencers, watermarks or logos (QC enforces it).
- Look at the preview of each device (docu/templates/previews/<theme>/<device>.jpg) and copy its gallery spec as the starting point.
- Explain on paper: most explanations are collages built from prints, cut-out props and typed strips that land on their words.
- Stamp the verdicts (CLAIMED, UNPROVEN...) and slam the punch words.
- Hard cuts; sound on every drop, stamp and slam.
