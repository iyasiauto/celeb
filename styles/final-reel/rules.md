# Final Reel - how it is edited

- Cold open: first 45-60 s fast - 1.5-2 s cuts, framed clips and photos, a rollcall on the count of names. Then slow: 5-8 s shots, soft dissolves.
- One person = castcard on their name -> the roles (framed clips 2-4 s, photos with slow pushes) -> the turn (photos, B-roll) -> ageclock on the age -> memoriam for the heaviest endings.
- About 30 % moving footage (framed borrowed clips + B-roll), 70 % pictures and graphics.
- Borrowed show / film clips: short (2-4 s), silent, inside the reelframe (or flipped / cropped), always under narration, never full screen.
- Every age at death is an ageclock or the castcard's age chip; every named place of death that matters gets a map.
- No interviews / talking heads, no thumbnails or memes, no logos or other channels' captions (QC people mode enforces it).
- Facts only as the script states them; never show a living person as dead.
