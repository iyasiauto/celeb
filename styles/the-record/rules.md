# The Record - how it is edited

- One docket (FILE 03 / 10) per act; the heading names the act, never a name or number the voice has not reached.
- Every claim is part of the record: rulings and statements as transcript pages (lines arrive as spoken, the key words marked), figures as tally rows with their SOURCE, terms as lexicon entries, the mechanism as a chain (gate, delay clock, block).
- Stock is never plain: it plays inside the casebox evidence viewer (exhibit number, timecode, caption); community footage full frame with a place line. Every clip under 5 s; longer lines are cut into short shots.
- About 30-38 % moving footage, the rest photographs and graphics; a graphic at least every 40 s.
- No faces of victims or private people, no influencers, vloggers, interviews or talking heads; lists and posters never show a real private name.
- Calm motion (pages drift <= 3.5 %, one stamp, one marker sweep); sound under the voice: riser + low hit on files, typewriter for typed lines, paper, stamps, soft whooshes.
