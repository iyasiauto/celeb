# Cold Case Files - how this template is edited

- Open on the case number and the date over a dark photo of the place.
- One evidence board that fills in, piece by piece, through the whole video (the recurring device).
- Every claim that is not proven gets a stamp: "UNCONFIRMED", "REPORTED", "DISPUTED".
- Timeline scene at every jump in time.
- 20 % footage, 3-6 s per scene, hard cuts, no dissolves.
