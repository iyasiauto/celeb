DESCRIPTION
Some faces stay frozen in time. They were children when we first met them — on a Saturday morning, in a movie we wore out on VHS, in a show the whole family watched together. Thirty-five of them never got the decades we assumed they had.

From Judith Barsi, the voice of Ducky in The Land Before Time, to River Phoenix, Cameron Boyce, Heather O'Rourke, Bobby Driscoll and Michelle Trachtenberg — we remember the roles that made them part of our childhoods, and the stories that ended far too soon. Some were taken by illness, some by violence, some by accidents, and too many by an industry that forgets its children the moment the ratings move on.

Which name stayed with you? Share it in the comments — every one of them deserves to be more than a headline.

If you or someone you know is struggling, please reach out to a local crisis line (in the US, call or text 988).
