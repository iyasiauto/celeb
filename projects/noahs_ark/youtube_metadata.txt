DESCRIPTION
In September 2026, researchers began drilling into a boat-shaped formation on the slopes of Mount Tendürek in eastern Turkey, and headlines asked whether Noah's Ark had finally been confirmed after 4,300 years. Nothing from the site has been dated yet. The 4,300-year figure comes from an archbishop's arithmetic in the 1650s.

This investigation sorts the 66-year Durupınar controversy into three baskets: what we know, what is claimed, and what remains unknown. It covers the 1959 aerial photo, the 1960 expedition, Ron Wyatt and David Fasold, the 2026 radar scans, soil tests and core drilling, and the peer-reviewed geology that explains the shape. It also covers what the laboratories can settle this winter.
