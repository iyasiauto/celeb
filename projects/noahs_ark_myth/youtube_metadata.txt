DESCRIPTION
On a mountainside in eastern Turkey the grass grows greener in the outline of a ship. For sixty-six years that outline has been photographed, dynamited, claimed and recanted. In September 2026 someone drilled into it for the first time.

This video follows the whole hunt, from the 1959 NATO aerial photographs and the 1960 expedition to Ron Wyatt, David Fasold's courtroom testimony and today's Noah's Ark Scans drilling programme. Then it puts the evidence on trial: the scans, the soil, the cores and the shattered drill bit, against the published geology. The verdict separates what is real, what is still a myth, and the laboratory tests that could settle it.
