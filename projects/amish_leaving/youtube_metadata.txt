DESCRIPTION
For three centuries Lancaster County, Pennsylvania has been the heartland of the Old Order Amish. Today hundreds of families are selling their homesteads and moving away. Most people assume the Amish way of life is fading. The opposite is true: the Amish population has grown from about 5,000 a century ago to more than 400,000 today, doubling roughly every 22 years, and their farmland has run out.

This documentary follows the numbers and the faith behind the move: why an 80-acre farm cannot be divided among five sons, how land prices of $25,000 to $35,000 an acre pushed young men off the soil and into workshops, why church leaders see farming as the wall that keeps the world outside, and how whole church districts are rebuilding farm life in Upstate New York, Kentucky and the Midwest.
