DESCRIPTION
On September 22, 2026, a researcher said his team was "one hundred percent" certain that the Durupınar formation in eastern Turkey is man-made. Their drilling began the next day. No laboratory has reported anything since.

This video takes each pillar of evidence in turn: the dimensions, the history, the radar scans, the soil and the new drill cores. For each one it separates three things headlines tend to blur: what was observed, what was claimed, and what has actually been confirmed. It covers the case for the site, the published geology against it, and what the lab results could settle this winter.
