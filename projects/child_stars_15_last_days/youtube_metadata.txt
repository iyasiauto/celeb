15 MOST FAMOUS Child Stars Who Died in the Last Few Days

ALTERNATIVE TITLES

DESCRIPTION
They were famous before they were old enough to sign their own contracts. Some of them were millionaires before middle school. And in what feels like the last few days, we have lost them, one after another.

From Hayden Panettiere, the invincible cheerleader of Heroes, to Malcolm-Jamal Warner, Theo Huxtable on The Cosby Show, Michelle Trachtenberg of Harriet the Spy and Gossip Girl, Liam Payne of One Direction, Aaron Carter and Kevin Clark of School of Rock: we remember the roles that made them part of our childhoods, and the stories that ended far too soon.

Which of them did you grow up with? Tell us in the comments.
